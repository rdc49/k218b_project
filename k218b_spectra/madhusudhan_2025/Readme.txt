Data from Madhusudhan et al. 2025: New Constraints on DMS and DMDS in the Atmosphere of K2-18 b from JWST MIRI 

These data correspond to the JWST transmission spectrum of K2-18 b reported in Fig. 4 of Madhusudhan et al. (2025), obtained with the JWST MIRI LRS instrument and reduced with two independent pipelines, JExoRES and JexoPipe. The data from the two pipeline are given in the two separate .txt files with the pipeline name in the filename. 

Also included are the corresponding retrieved posterior distributions for the canonical atmospheric model with both DMS and DMDS included.
